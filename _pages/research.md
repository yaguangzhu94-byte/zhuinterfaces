<section class="research-direction" id="interfaces">
  <div class="direction-intro">
    <div class="direction-number">01</div>
    <div class="direction-label">ELECTROCHEMICAL INTERFACES</div>
    <h2>Dynamic interfaces during electrochemical transformations.</h2>
    <p>We investigate working electrochemical interfaces where catalysts, electrolytes, gases, ions, and phases continuously reorganize. Carbon dioxide conversion and ammonia oxidation serve as model systems for connecting interfacial evolution with transport, selectivity, and stability.</p>
  </div>

<section class="research-direction" id="transport">
  <div class="direction-intro">
    <div class="direction-number">02</div>
    <div class="direction-label">2D MATERIALS & CONFINED TRANSPORT</div>
    <h2>Engineering 2D materials to control transport under confinement.</h2>
    <p>We synthesize and assemble laminar two-dimensional materials with tunable interlayer chemistry and mesoscale organization. The central question is how hydration, confinement, defects, and orientational order collectively determine selective ion transport.</p>
